What You Need to Qualify

Getting your car financed is straightforward. Here are the core things you need to prepare:

01

Down Payment (Deposit)

Usually 20% to 30% of the total vehicle cost (CIF value plus local duty charges).

02

Steady Income Proof

Last 3 to 6 months bank statements showing regular salary or business cash flow.

03

Official ID & KRA PIN

Copy of your National ID/Passport and active KRA PIN certificate for tax compliance verification.
